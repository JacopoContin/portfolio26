Managers could see scheduled hours and a gross wage estimate. What they could not see was the real number: pensions, insurance, and what actually left the account on payday.

Nory Pay embedded payroll into the same system that builds the rota, so the cost a manager sees while scheduling is the cost the business ends up paying.
