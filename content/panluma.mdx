An agent that can act on your behalf breaks most of the interface conventions we rely on. There is no obvious place to see what it understood, no natural moment to correct it, and no established shape for asking permission.

These are working prototypes rather than mockups, because this is the kind of problem you cannot evaluate from a static screen.
