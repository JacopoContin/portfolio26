## 01. Context

Panluma brings projects, CRM, finance and operations into one agentic app. The question: **what does business software look like when an agent is the primary interface?**

The user is "Solo Sam", founder of a 1 to 50 person software agency juggling delivery, clients and finance across disconnected tools. I owned **product strategy, IA and agent interaction design**, prototyping directly in code.

## 03. Delegating without losing sight

<Callout label="Problem">

Hand work to an agent, especially by voice, and you lose sight of it. It says "on it" and the work happens somewhere you can't see.

</Callout>

Both patterns share one principle: **the agent works in your interface, where you can watch it.** They run the same flow:

1. **Import the project from Slack.** Sam pastes the brief or says "set up the Trailmix launch". The agent checks team load and proposes six tasks with owners and estimates.
2. **Create the project and tasks.** After approval, the agent takes over the Work page: a glowing border, a status bar, rows filling in one by one.
3. **Modify.** "Maya needs more time on the wireframes." The agent highlights the cell and moves the date, checking nothing downstream slips.
4. **Update the client.** It drafts the reply in Priya's Inbox thread. Nothing is sent without approval of the exact text.

**Take back** returns the page to Sam at any step.

---

## 04. Pattern 1: Chat

The handover is an **approval card**: "I'll take over the Work page to create the project and its 6 tasks. You can take it back at any time."

Then the conversation **moves to a side panel**, listing each step as it runs. Owners and estimates land marked **Suggested**, so Sam knows what to check. Follow-up chips keep the next move one tap away.

## 05. Pattern 2: Voice

Voice has nothing to read, so every **tool call appears as an action beside the live transcript**, with its status.

## 06. On mobile

On the phone, voice is the default. Sam holds to talk, and PanLuma **drives the app while it works**: it opens Tasks, creates the project, splits the work by who has room, then reports back. It still **asks before emailing the client**.

## 07. Making the agent's work legible

<Callout label="Principle">

Visibility doesn't limit autonomy. **You delegate more when you can see what the agent did, is doing and will do next.**

</Callout>

- **Active row and field states** show exactly where the agent is working.
- **Suggested vs pending values** separate AI guesses from data still to come.
- **Stable layouts** keep the page from shifting while the agent writes into it.
- **Separate task and agent statuses** avoid confusing AI activity with task progress.
- **Reduced-motion support** keeps every state readable without the animated border.
